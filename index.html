About

Fuel Data — Fleet Fuel & Margin Tracker

Fuel Data helps fleet owners and dispatchers log every fuel purchase in seconds and see exactly where money is going. Each entry is tied to a vehicle plate and calculated instantly for litres, spend and margin, giving an accurate, real-time picture of fuel cost and profitability across the fleet. All records stay private on this device, with built-in export and backup tools so your data is never locked in.
